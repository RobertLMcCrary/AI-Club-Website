Students developed an AI-related project in a week, then presented it on judging day. Featuring guest speaker Ravitez Dondeti, in collaboration with CCSE.

Rules

  1. Projects must be completed within the hackathon period.
  2. All projects must utilize AI in some capacity.
  3. You must be a KSU student to participate.
