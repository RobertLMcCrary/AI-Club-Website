$ man hackathon

                    
FAQ.

                

                

                    

                        Who can participate?
                        
All KSU students, any major and any skill level. Students from other colleges can register too.

                    

                    

                        Do I need a team?
                        
No. You can go solo or form a team of up to 4.

                    

                    

                        I’ve never used AI to build anything. Can I still join?
                        
Yes. Every skill level is welcome. The hackathon is a good place to learn.

                    

                    

                        What should I build?
                        
Something that uses AI to help students on campus: the dining hall, parking, study rooms, or any other campus problem you want to solve. Check the tracks for the cybersecurity and game options.

                    

                    

                        How do I register?
                        
Fill out the registration form. You don’t need OwlLife.

                    

                    

                        Can I enter more than one track?
                        
Yes. You can compete in all of them if you want, but you need to present your project for every track you enter.

                    

                    

                        How does the week work?
                        
Monday is kickoff. Tuesday through Thursday you build, and Wednesday the judges are around to help you with your project. Friday is demos, judging and awards.

                    

                    

                        Is there food?
                        
Yes, free food on opening day (Monday) and closing day (Friday).

                    

                    

                        Will there be people from industry?
                        
Yes. People from Amazon, Google, Microsoft and Uber are coming to the hackathon.

                    

                    

                        What should I bring?
                        
A laptop, your charger, and an idea (or come find one).

                    

                    

                        I have another question.
                        
Ask in the KSU AI Club Discord.
